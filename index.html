Internal tooling

Odyn Cars

PurposeMoving vehicle listing footage from storage to a video channel on a fixed schedule.
Operated byOne person, on accounts they own.
AvailabilityNot a product. Nothing to sign up for.
InterfaceNone. It runs from a scheduler and writes to a log file.

Vehicle footage is recorded at inspection, deposited in a storage folder, and published without further handling. The software exists so that step does not need a person at a keyboard each morning.

Enquiries about a particular vehicle, or about this software, go to odyncars@gmail.com.

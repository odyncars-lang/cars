Internal tooling

How this software handles data

Odyn Cars · revised 7 November 2026

  1. There are no users besides the operator

    This is a scheduled job run by one person against accounts that person owns. Nobody signs up, nothing is collected from visitors to this page, and no cookies are set by it.

  2. What the software is permitted to touch

    Two permissions are requested when the operator authorises the job, and they are used for nothing else:

    • Storage access, limited in practice to one folder of pending footage and one folder where published footage is filed afterwards.
    • Video upload, used to publish that footage to the operator's own channel.

    Mail, contacts, calendars and the remainder of the operator's storage are never read.

  3. Footage is not kept

    A file is fetched to the machine running the job, published, and erased from that machine in the same pass. Nothing is archived locally and nothing is copied anywhere else.

  4. Nothing leaves for a third party

    The job talks to the storage and video services and to nothing else. There is no analytics, no advertising network, no tracking and no onward transfer of any kind.

  5. Credentials

    Authorisation tokens sit in files on the machine running the job, restricted to the system account that executes it. Their only use is proving to the service provider that the job may act for the operator.

  6. What is written down

    A plain text log on the operator's own machine records filenames, timestamps and any errors. There is no database and no personal information in it.

  7. Withdrawing permission

    The operator can revoke access at any moment from the account permissions page. Revocation takes effect immediately and the job then fails rather than continuing.

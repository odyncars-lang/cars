Privacy Policy

Odyn Cars · Last updated 7 November 2026

Scope

Odyn Cars is a private automation tool operated by a single individual on their own Google account. It has no users other than its operator, offers no sign-up, and collects no information from anyone else.

Data the application accesses

When authorised, the application accesses the operator's own Google account for two purposes:

  • Google Drive — to list files in a designated source folder, download those video files, and move them to an archive folder after processing.
  • YouTube — to upload those video files to the operator's own YouTube channel.

The application does not read email, contacts, calendars, photos, or any Drive content outside the two folders it is configured to use.

How data is handled

Video files are downloaded to the machine running the application, uploaded to YouTube, and deleted from that machine immediately afterwards. No copy is retained.

OAuth credentials issued by Google are stored in local token files on the machine running the application, readable only by the operating system account that runs it. They are used solely to authenticate to Google's own APIs.

Data sharing

No data is shared with, sold to, or transmitted to any third party. The application communicates only with Google's APIs. There is no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. This website sets no cookies.

Retention

The application maintains no database and stores no personal information. A local plain-text log records file names and timestamps of processing runs, retained on the operator's own machine.

Revoking access

Authorisation can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately prevents all further access.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to odyncars@gmail.com.
